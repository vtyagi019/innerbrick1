Custom Engraving & Craftsmanship Details

The box is cut from a single sheet and folded on a living hinge -a spine made of a hundred fine laser cuts, with no screws and no glue. That is why it opens flat, closes flush and keeps its shape for years.

  • Deep precision engraving: the Ganesha, Om and Shubh–Labh motifs are engraved and cut through the front panel, so the artwork reads in shadow as well as in ink-free contrast.
  • Personalisation: a family name, wedding date, monogram or a short blessing can be engraved inside the lid, on the base or along the front edge.
  • Hand-sanded finish: every edge is sanded by hand so the box feels smooth where fingers actually touch it, then sealed with a matte food-safe oil.
  • Sturdy living hinge: the folded spine is rated for thousands of open-and-close cycles; the lid holds its tension without a latch.
  • Approval first: you see the engraving layout as a digital mockup on WhatsApp before the laser is switched on.

Best Gifting Occasions for Ganesha Keepsake Boxes

Because it is both a blessing and a container, the box works anywhere a gift needs to hold something -or to become the keepsake itself.

  • Wedding invitations & return favours: a small engraved box with the couple's names and date, holding sweets, a laddoo, a coin or a thank-you card.
  • Griha Pravesh gifts: a housewarming present that carries a blessing and can be reused for house keys, jewellery or pooja items.
  • Raksha Bandhan & Diwali hampers: a luxury festive hamper base that looks considered rather than bought at the last minute.
  • Milestone anniversaries: first-year jewellery, letters, a watch or a ring, with the date engraved inside the lid.
  • Corporate & institutional gifting: branded boxes for client Diwali gifts, with the company mark engraved on the front.

Dimensions, Materials & Delivery

The standard box measures roughly 150 × 110 × 45 mm and is cut from 4 mm birch-finish ply, which is light enough to courier but rigid enough to keep its edges crisp. The interior depth takes jewellery, rakhis, folded letters, invitation cards or a small idol. Larger and smaller sizes are produced on request, including a slim invitation box.

Production takes 3–5 business days after you approve the layout, plus shipping. Delhi NCR orders are usually delivered within a week; pan-India tracked courier takes 2–5 days after dispatch. Boxes are wrapped in soft paper, corner-protected and packed with the hinge supported so it cannot crease in transit.

Yes. Names, a wedding date, a short blessing or a monogram can be engraved on the inside of the lid, the base or the front panel. Send the exact wording on WhatsApp and we will show you the layout in a digital mockup before cutting.
