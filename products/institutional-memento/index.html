Built for Universities, Conferences, Corporates & Government Bodies

An institutional memento has a different job from a gift. It has to read correctly on a stage, survive a flight in a suitcase, and still look substantial on a shelf five years later. That is why we engrave deep rather than print, and why every batch is proofed before production.

College convocations, seminars & guest-of-honor plaques

Graduating batches, best-outgoing-student awards, faculty felicitations and visiting dignitaries. The college crest is engraved in relief, the citation set in a clean serif, and the recipient name engraved individually so nobody receives a generic plate.

Corporate milestone awards & leadership trophies

Work anniversaries, sales achievers, project launches, retirements and client appreciation. We can match your brand colours with a stained or matte-finished wood, and pair the plaque with a carved stand for a heavier tabletop presence.

Custom logo & individual recipient name engraving

Send a vector logo and a spreadsheet of names. We typeset every citation, share a single proof showing the layout and one sample name, then run the batch with each name engraved individually. Spelling is confirmed by you in writing before cutting.

Institutional Memento FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity for institutional mementos?

There is no minimum for a single award or guest-of-honour plaque. Volume pricing begins at 10 pieces, and we regularly produce runs of 25 to 500 for convocations, conferences and corporate award nights. Every piece in a batch can carry a different recipient name.

What logo file formats do you accept?

Vector files are ideal: SVG, AI, EPS or PDF. A high-resolution PNG or JPG at 300 dpi also works for simple logos. If the only file available is a low-resolution image, we redraw the mark in vector before engraving and show you the proof.

Can you meet a short event deadline?

Usually yes. Standard production is 3–7 business days after proof approval. If your conference date is closer, tell us the date first: we hold express slots for small runs and can dispatch within 48 hours in Delhi NCR when materials are in stock.

Do you provide GST invoices and purchase order support?

Yes. We issue a GST invoice with our GSTIN and accept purchase orders from colleges, companies and government bodies. The standard terms are 50% advance to begin production and the balance before dispatch, paid by NEFT, UPI or bank transfer.
