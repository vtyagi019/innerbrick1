Which custom gift should you choose?

A quick way to decide: figures are for people, panels are for walls and shrines, boxes are for things worth keeping, and mementos are for institutions that need to thank someone in public.

Custom 3D figures and miniatures

Best when the gift is a portrait. We sculpt from a front-facing photograph, print the model in resin or PLA, hand-clean the supports and finish it. Couples, pets, mascots and desk bobbleheads all work well.

Photo-depth deity relief panels

Best for a home mandir, a pooja room wall or a Griha Pravesh gift. The artwork is engraved at varying physical depths so daylight or a warm spotlight creates shadow and highlight across the carving.

Engraved Ganesha keepsake boxes

Best for weddings, return favors and Diwali hampers. A living-hinge box with a cut-through Ganesha front, personalised with names, dates or a short blessing inside the lid.

Institutional and corporate mementos

Best for convocations, conferences, farewells and award nights. Logos, citation text, dates and individual recipient names engraved on a stained wood plate, with volume pricing from ten pieces.
