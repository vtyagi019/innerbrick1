Popular Custom 3D Figurine Styles

Most orders fall into one of three shapes. If yours does not, that is usually the interesting one.

Solo & couple portrait miniatures

Ideal for birthdays, anniversaries and graduations. We model the pose from your photograph, keep the outfit and hairstyle recognisable, and print at 5", 7" or 9" tall so the face still reads clearly at desk distance.

Custom bobbleheads & desk collectibles

Fun, personalized gifts for colleagues, bosses and farewells. The head is modelled slightly larger than life, mounted on a spring or fixed base, and finished in a matte or satin seal that survives an office desk.

Pet miniatures & custom brand mascots

3D sculpted keepsakes of pets or company mascots. Send two or three angles of the animal, or a flat vector of your mascot, and we will turn it into a printable model with a matching base.

How Photo-to-3D Sculpting Works at InnerBrick

The process is deliberately slow at the start and fast at the end, because a bad sculpt cannot be rescued by a good printer.

  • Photo requirements: one clear front-facing photo, taken in daylight, with the face unobstructed. A side photo helps with noses, ears and hairlines.
  • Sculpt and preview: we build the model, then send a 3D render on WhatsApp. You can ask for changes — a different pose, a hat removed, a pet added — before anything is printed.
  • Printing: resin for fine facial detail, PLA for larger and more robust pieces. Layer height is chosen per model, not per batch.
  • Finishing: supports are removed by hand, seams are sanded, and the figure is sealed. Optional hand-painting is quoted separately.
  • Sizes: 80–150 mm is the sweet spot for a single figure. Couples and groups are usually built on one shared base.

Materials, Care & Delivery

Every figure is a display piece, not a toy. Resin holds the sharpest detail and is best for faces and small lettering; PLA is lighter and more forgiving for larger mascots. Keep the figure out of direct sunlight and away from heat sources such as a car dashboard or a window sill in summer, and dust it with a dry soft cloth rather than water. Handled that way, the finish will outlast the occasion it was made for.

Orders are packed in foam-lined, shock-proof boxes and shipped with tracking across India. Delhi NCR addresses are usually delivered by hand or same-week courier; everywhere else takes 2–5 days after dispatch. Because we quote before printing, you will always know the landed cost before you commit.

Custom 3D Figure FAQs

What kind of photo do you need to make a custom 3D figure?

One clear, well-lit front-facing photo where the face is not covered by sunglasses, a mask or hair. A second side-profile photo helps with noses and hairlines, and group photos are fine as long as each face is sharp. Send the originals on WhatsApp rather than a screenshot.

How long does a custom 3D printed figure take?

Allow 4–6 business days for sculpting, your preview approval and printing, plus 2–5 days for tracked shipping. Delhi NCR deliveries usually arrive sooner. Tell us your deadline on WhatsApp and we will confirm whether a rush slot is available before you pay.

How durable is the figure and how should I care for it?

Figures are printed in resin or PLA, hand-cleaned, assembled and sealed. Dust with a dry soft cloth, keep them out of direct sunlight and away from heat, and treat them as a display piece rather than a toy for small children.

How much does a custom 3D figure cost?

Custom 3D figures start from ₹649. The final price depends on height, the number of figures, the amount of custom modelling and the finish you choose. We quote on WhatsApp before any printing begins, so you can approve the price along with the preview.
