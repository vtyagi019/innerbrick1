Why Photo-Depth Relief Carving Outshines Flat Temple Frames

A printed frame gives you colour on a flat surface. A relief panel gives you a surface that changes as the light moves, because the artwork is not printed -it is carved to different physical depths and the shadows do the shading.

  • Real tactile depth: robes, jewellery and facial features sit at different heights, so you can trace the carving with a fingertip.
  • Zero ink fading: there is no pigment to bleach in sunlight. The contrast comes from geometry and a matte sealed wood finish.
  • Architectural presence: a framed print sits on a wall; a relief panel belongs to it. The carved edge and tonal wood suit both a modern pooja niche and a traditional mandir.
  • Light-responsive: a warm spotlight at an angle creates a dramatically different image from soft morning daylight, which is why the same panel never looks stale.
  • Made from your reference: send a photograph of your family deity or a favourite artwork and we will work from that, not from a stock image library.

Recommended Lighting, Placement & Care

Relief carving is a lighting product as much as a wood product. A warm 2700–3000K spotlight placed 30–45° above and to one side, roughly 30–60 cm away, produces the deepest shadow and the strongest sense of depth. A small picture light or an adjustable LED arm is ideal; direct overhead white light flattens the carving, and a window with morning light does the job for free.

Hang the centre of the panel at eye level when standing, or slightly above the idols if it sits behind a mandir. Every panel ships with a keyhole or sawtooth hanger fitted, plus screws and wall plugs; larger pieces use a French cleat and should be fixed into a stud or masonry plug. Dust with a dry, soft cloth only -no wet wipes, no spray polish -and keep the panel out of direct afternoon sun so the sealed finish stays even.

Deity Relief Panel FAQs

What lighting shows a deity relief panel at its best?

A warm 2700–3000K spotlight placed 30–45 degrees above and to one side, about 30–60 cm from the panel, gives the deepest shadow. A small picture light or an LED arm works well; daylight from a nearby window is even better. Avoid flat white ceiling light directly overhead, which flattens the relief.

Which deities can you carve, and what sizes are available?

Ganesha, Shiva, Krishna, Lakshmi, Durga, Sai Baba and most other deities, provided we have a clear artwork or photograph to work from. Our standard panel is about 200 × 240 mm, and custom sizes up to roughly 600 × 900 mm are quoted per design.

Does the panel come with wall-mounting hardware?

Yes. Every panel leaves the workshop with a keyhole or sawtooth hanger fitted and the matching screws and wall plugs in the box. Larger panels use a French cleat. A tabletop stand can be added on request.

How is a relief panel packed for shipping?

Each panel is wrapped in soft foam, corner-protected and floated inside a double-wall carton so the carving never touches the box. We ship tracked and insured across India, with same-week delivery in Delhi NCR.
