Why InnerBrick

Why We Carve Instead of Just Print: The InnerBrick Difference

Most personalized gift shops print flat ink onto mugs or plaques that fade over time. At InnerBrick, we combine precision photo-depth relief carving with layered 3D printing so physical contours, shadows, and ambient light bring every portrait and deity carving to life.

That means two very different machines working to the same end: a laser that reads greyscale as depth and burns it into wood a pass at a time, and a printer that builds a figure a fraction of a millimetre at a time. Both are slow. Both leave something that cannot be reprinted identically.

Ordering

How to Order Your Custom Gift in 3 Simple Steps

No account, no cart, no checkout maze. Most orders begin with a single WhatsApp message and a photograph.

01

1. Share Your Photo, Logo, or Idea on WhatsApp

Send one clear front-facing photo, your logo file or a paragraph describing the idea. Tell us the occasion, the date you need it by and the budget you have in mind.

02

2. Approve Your 3D Digital Preview Before Production

We reply with a sketch or 3D render on WhatsApp. Nothing is cut or printed until you approve it, so there are no surprises when the parcel arrives.

03

3. Precision Crafting & Safe Doorstep Delivery

Your piece is engraved or printed, hand-finished, sealed and packed in shock-proof packaging, then tracked to your door anywhere in India.
