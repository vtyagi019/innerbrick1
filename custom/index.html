Custom Order FAQs

What file types can I send for a custom order?

Photos as JPG, PNG or HEIC; artwork and logos as PDF, SVG, AI or EPS; and handwritten sketches as a clear phone photo. WhatsApp compresses images, so send the original file by email if it is a large photograph.

How many revisions do I get on the digital mockup?

Two rounds of changes are included on every mockup -edits to the pose, wording, layout or size. Further structural changes after production has started are charged at the modelling rate, which is why we do not cut anything until you approve the preview in writing.

Can you take rush orders?

Often, yes. Standard turnaround is 3–7 business days depending on the piece. Tell us the date you need it by on WhatsApp and we will confirm whether an express slot is free before you order. Delhi NCR delivery can usually be arranged a day before the occasion.

What are the payment terms for a custom order?

Personal orders are paid 100% in advance once the mockup is approved. Institutional and bulk orders are 50% advance to start production and the balance before dispatch, with a GST invoice. We accept UPI, NEFT and bank transfer.
