Bulk & institutional enquiries

For conferences, convocations, corporate award nights and wedding favours, send these four things in your first message and we can quote in one reply:

  • 1. Quantity (a single piece is fine -volume pricing starts at 10)
  • 2. Event date and delivery city
  • 3. Logo or crest in SVG, AI, EPS, PDF or 300 dpi PNG
  • 4. Recipient names list, plus the citation or award wording

We reply with a layout proof timeline, slab pricing and the last dispatch date that still makes your event. GST invoice and purchase-order support included.

What happens after you message

Every enquiry is answered by the person who will actually make the piece, usually within a few working hours. If you are sending a photo, send the original rather than a screenshot -sharper files mean a sharper sculpt. We confirm size, material and price, share a mockup, and only then start production. For Delhi NCR handovers we can usually agree a delivery day in the same week; for everywhere else we quote the courier window before you pay. Quotes are free, and there is no obligation to order after seeing one.
