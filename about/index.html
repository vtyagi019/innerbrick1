About InnerBrick: Why We Carve Instead of Just Print

InnerBrick started in a small workshop with a laser, a spool of filament and a stubborn belief that a gift should outlive the occasion. Generic gifts get used. Personal ones get kept.

The problem with a bought gift

Most gifting is a transaction. A box off a shelf, wrapped the same way for a hundred other people, opened once and forgotten by the next month. The engraving, if there is one, is a thin layer of ink or a printed sticker that fades where a thumb rests.

What we do instead

We work in layers. The laser reads an image as depth and burns it into wood a pass at a time; the printer builds a figure a fraction of a millimetre at a time. Both are slow, and both leave something that cannot be reprinted identically — the grain, the depth, the small variations of a hand-finished edge.

That is the difference between a printed surface and a carved one. Ink sits on top of the material. Relief carving removes it, so the light itself becomes part of the artwork: a deity panel in morning daylight looks nothing like the same panel under a warm evening spotlight.

Dual-technology mastery

Two machines, two very different disciplines, one studio. Our photo-depth relief carving maps greyscale to physical depth, then engraves in multiple passes until the shading reads correctly from an angle. Our 3D printing side is sculpting-led: a model is built for the photograph, printed in resin or PLA, then cleaned, assembled, sanded and sealed by hand.

Having both under one roof means we recommend the right process rather than the one we happen to own. A portrait that would look flat on wood becomes a figure; a logo that would be fragile as a print becomes a deep-engraved plaque.

Quality assurance, piece by piece

Every order is quoted, cut and checked by hand before it ships. We proof the layout or 3D render on WhatsApp first, inspect the finished piece under raking light for chips, uneven depth or missed detail, then seal and pack it in foam-lined, corner-protected packaging. Nothing leaves the table on a Friday afternoon without being looked at twice.

Delhi NCR roots, pan-India reach

InnerBrick is run by Vaanshit Tyagi out of Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. That puts us within reach of Noida, Delhi, Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Gurugram for same-week local delivery, while tracked courier covers the rest of India. It is also why we answer on WhatsApp rather than a ticket queue — the person who quotes your piece is the person who makes it.

The living-hinge box — the piece that convinced us this was worth doing properly.

If you can describe it, we can probably carve it.
